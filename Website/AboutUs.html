About Eco Trackers

Key Details

  • The motivation is to quantify carbon footprints and explore pathways to carbon neutrality for Indian coal mines.
  • Founded by a passionate team of environmentalists, technologists, and industry experts who recognized the urgent need for change in the mining sector.
  • Combines experience in environmental science, data analytics, and mining operations to address unique challenges.

Our Mission

  • Estimate Carbon Emissions
  • Carbon Sink Estimation
  • Data Visualization
  • Carbon Neutrality Pathways

Our Vision

  • Promoting Environmental Sustainability
  • Empowering Stakeholders
  • Fostering Community Engagement
  • Driving Economic Efficiency
