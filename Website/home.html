Eco-friendly solutions for a greener mining future.

🌍 Environmental Impact

Coal mining operations release large volumes of greenhouse gases and pollutants into the atmosphere.

  • CO₂ Emissions: Millions of tons yearly
  • Methane (CH₄): 25× more potent than CO₂
  • Pollution: Air and water contamination damages ecosystems

🌱 Solutions to Reduce Emissions

  1. Carbon Capture: Trap CO₂ before it enters the atmosphere
  2. Renewable Energy: Use solar and wind to power operations
  3. Sustainable Mining: Eco-conscious tech and land rehabilitation
